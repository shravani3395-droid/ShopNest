Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how ShopNest handles information when you use our website.

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ShopNest. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you use the ShopNest website.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide when creating an account, placing an order, contacting customer support or using certain features of the website.

This information may include your name, email address, phone number, delivery address and order details.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through ShopNest may be used to process orders, provide customer support, manage accounts, improve website functionality and provide a better shopping experience.

4. Account Information

When you create a ShopNest account, you should provide accurate information. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your account credentials.

5. Payment Information

Payment information may be processed through appropriate payment services. Payment details should be entered only through secure payment processes provided during checkout.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

ShopNest may use cookies or browser storage to remember preferences, maintain shopping cart information and improve website functionality.

7. Data Protection

We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with the website from unauthorized access or misuse. However, no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Sharing of Information

ShopNest does not intend to sell personal information to third parties. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to provide services such as payment processing, delivery and customer support.

9. Third-Party Websites

ShopNest may contain links to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

10. User Rights

Users may review or update certain account information and may contact ShopNest support regarding questions about their personal information.

11. Changes to This Policy

ShopNest may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be displayed on this page along with the updated date.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our customer support team.
