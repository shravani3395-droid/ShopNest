Frequently Asked Questions

How can I track my order?

You can check your order status from the Orders section of your ShopNest account.

How can I return a product?

Open your order details and select the return option if the product is eligible for return.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery time depends on your location, product availability and selected delivery option.

What payment methods are available?

ShopNest can support multiple payment options depending on your checkout setup, including online payment methods.

How can I change my account information?

Visit your Account page to manage your available account information and settings.

What should I do if I receive a damaged product?

Contact ShopNest Customer Service with your order information so the issue can be reviewed.
