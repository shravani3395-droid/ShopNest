UPI Payment Help

My UPI payment failed. What should I do?

Check your internet connection, verify your payment details and try the payment again. If the amount was deducted, check the payment status before attempting another payment.

Money was deducted but my order was not confirmed

Check your order status and payment status. If the transaction remains unresolved, contact ShopNest Customer Service.

Payment Failed

Why did my payment fail?

Payments can fail because of incorrect payment details, temporary network issues, bank restrictions or payment-service problems.

Can I try the payment again?

Yes. After confirming that the previous transaction was unsuccessful, you can try another supported payment method.

Payment Refunds

How do payment refunds work?

Once a refund is approved, the refund is processed according to the applicable payment method and ShopNest order process.

My refund has not arrived

Check your order and refund status first. If the refund remains pending beyond the expected processing period, contact ShopNest support.
