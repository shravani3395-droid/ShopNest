Delivery Dates

How is my delivery date calculated?

Estimated delivery depends on product availability, delivery location, shipping option and order processing time.

Can my delivery date change?

In some situations, delivery estimates can change because of inventory, transportation or other delivery-related factors.

Shipping Information

Where does ShopNest deliver?

Delivery availability depends on the product, seller and delivery location entered during checkout.

Are shipping charges applicable?

Shipping charges, if applicable, may depend on the product, delivery location and selected delivery option.

Can I change my delivery address?

Address changes depend on the order status. Check your order information as soon as possible if you need to update the delivery address.

Delayed or Missing Delivery

What should I do if my order is delayed?

Check your Orders page for the latest delivery information. If the order remains delayed, contact ShopNest Customer Service.

My package says delivered but I did not receive it

Check the delivery information and confirm that the package was not received by another household member or at an expected delivery location. If you still cannot locate it, contact support.

What if my package arrives damaged?

Keep the product and packaging information available and contact ShopNest Customer Service for assistance.
