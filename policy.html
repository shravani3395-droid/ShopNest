Privacy Policy

ShopNest respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ShopNest. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you use our website and online shopping services.

2. Information We Collect

When you use ShopNest, we may collect information that you provide while creating an account, placing an order or contacting customer support.

This may include your name, email address, phone number, delivery address and order information.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through ShopNest may be used to process orders, provide customer support, improve our website and communicate important information about your orders.

4. Account Information

If you create a ShopNest account, you are responsible for keeping your login information secure. Please avoid sharing your password or other account credentials with others.

5. Payment Information

Payment information may be processed through appropriate payment services. Users should ensure that payment details are entered only on trusted and secure payment pages.

6. Cookies and Website Data

ShopNest may use cookies, browser storage or similar technologies to remember preferences, maintain shopping cart information and improve the overall website experience.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the website. However, no online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Information Sharing

ShopNest does not intend to sell personal information to third parties. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to provide services such as payment processing, delivery and customer support.

9. Third-Party Services

ShopNest may use third-party services for certain website functions. These services may have their own privacy policies and practices.

10. Children's Privacy

ShopNest is intended for general online shopping use. Users should provide personal information only when they are permitted to do so under applicable rules and with appropriate supervision where required.

11. Your Privacy Choices

Users may review and update certain account information through their ShopNest account. If you have questions about your information, you can contact ShopNest customer support.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

ShopNest may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page along with the updated date.

13. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the ShopNest customer support team.
